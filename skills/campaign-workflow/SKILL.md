# Campaign Workflow

Campaign generation is optional and user-triggered. The user does not want automatic fixed-duration campaigns or default campaign calendars.

## Discovery
Ask for product/service, objective, audience, positioning, proof points, channels, brand profile, budget constraints if relevant, required assets, timing (only if the user has a real schedule), approval owners, and success measures.

## Strategy
Define:
- Campaign objective and audience.
- Core message and supporting messages.
- Positioning and proof points.
- Funnel stage and intended audience action.
- Channel roles and content variations.
- Measurement plan and assumptions.

## Production
The campaign may focus mainly on LinkedIn. Produce only the assets the user requests or approves, such as LinkedIn posts, graphics, carousels, presentation assets, or ads. Adapt across multiple platforms only when explicitly requested.

Generate multiple creative directions when useful, compare them against the brief, and recommend the strongest. Do not produce a 7-day or 30-day plan unless the user specifies a duration.

## Governance and QA
Do not publish, schedule, send, or spend budget without explicit approval and supported tools. Validate factual claims, brand compliance, message consistency, accessibility, and asset rights. Provide a deliverables checklist and report incomplete items.
